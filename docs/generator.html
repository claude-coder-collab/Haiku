HinterGenerator

Sudoku Technique Practice

Generates puzzles in your browser where the first technique beyond singles, locked candidates and subsets (among the fish, wings and turbot fish offered here) is one you pick. Runs the HoDoKu generator and solver locally; nothing is uploaded.

Turbot fish

Rarer techniques can take minutes. Mutant fish without fins are almost never the first step, so expect to stop early.
