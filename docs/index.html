Sudoku Fish Practice

Generates puzzles in your browser where the first technique beyond singles, locked candidates and subsets is the fish you pick. Runs the HoDoKu generator and solver locally; nothing is uploaded.

Fish to practise

Rarer fish can take minutes. Mutant fish without fins are almost never the first step, so expect to stop early.

Export

Built from HoDoKu 2.2.0 by Bernhard Hobiger (GPLv3), compiled to WebAssembly with TeaVM. Source and build scripts are in this repository.
